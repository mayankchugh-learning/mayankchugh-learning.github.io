## Key lines to hit exactly (don't paraphrase on camera)

- "A Solution Architect solves a problem within a system. An Enterprise Architect decides which systems should exist at all."
- "The patterns travel across industries; the code never does."
- "Neither one is more important than the other — they're different altitudes of the same profession."
