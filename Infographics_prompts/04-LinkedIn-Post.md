Ask three people what an "Enterprise Architect" actually does, and you'll get three different answers.

A developer says: someone who draws diagrams nobody reads.
A CIO says: whoever owns the technology roadmap.
A consultant says: hands you a TOGAF slide with forty boxes on it.

None of them are wrong. That's the problem.

After 20+ years moving from technical/solution delivery (Coforge, TCS) into enterprise architecture (Capgemini, Deloitte) — across aviation, banking, and luxury retail — here's the distinction I'd give my younger self:

→ A Solution Architect solves a problem within a system.
→ An Enterprise Architect decides which systems should exist at all — and how that ties back to business strategy.

Same profession. Different altitude.
