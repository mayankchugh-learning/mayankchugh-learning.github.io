          Station 7

          In maths, a vector is an ordered list of numbers

          Say this: “School draws that list as an arrow. It has components, a length, and a direction. The embedding is the same kind of arrow. It just has 384 components, so we compare direction with the dot product instead of drawing it.”

          Vector A Vector B
          School nameIn this lesson
          ComponentOne number in the list. The Peak has 2. MiniLM has 384.
          DimensionHow many components. Two you can draw. 384 you only compare.
          MagnitudeLength of the arrow. Cosine divides it out, so a short sentence can match a long one.
          DirectionWhich way the arrow points. On the meaning map, direction is the topic.
          Dot productMultiply matching positions and add. That sum is the top of the cosine fraction. Search uses this. The cross product from class is a different operation.

          A right angle gives dot product 0. On the meaning map, that is two different topics.
