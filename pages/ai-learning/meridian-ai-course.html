How I use the slides

  • Right arrow or Space moves forward. Left arrow moves back. The first press on a slide reveals it step by step.
  • M opens the chapter menu. Escape closes it. N opens the explanation under the slide.
  • F is full screen. On a slide with buttons or sliders, click outside that box before the arrow keys change slides.
