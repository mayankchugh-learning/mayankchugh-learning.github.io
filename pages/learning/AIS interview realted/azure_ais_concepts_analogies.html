Plain language analogies for eight Azure integration and monitoring concepts, each paired with an everyday comparison to build intuition

Logic Apps

A recipe card that runs itself

Like a smart home routine: "when the front door opens, turn on the lights, then send a text." No one writes code — you just wire up trigger and steps visually.

Service Bus

A post office, not a phone call

A queue is a single mailbox — one letter, one recipient. A topic is a newsletter — one letter, copied and delivered to every subscriber. If no one's home, the letter waits safely instead of vanishing.

Event Grid

A fire alarm, not a delivery van

It doesn't carry cargo — it just shouts "something happened!" the instant it happens, and whoever's listening decides what to do. No payload to process, just a tap on the shoulder.

API Management

A building's front desk and security

Every visitor checks in at the desk, not directly into offices. The desk checks your badge (auth), limits how many people rush in at once (rate limiting), and keeps a visitor log (monitoring).

Functions

A light that only draws power when it's on

You don't pay for a light bulb sitting idle in a room no one enters. It switches on for one task, does the job, switches off. No server humming in the background 24/7.

Hybrid connectivity

A private road vs the public highway

VPN Gateway is an encrypted lane on the public highway — cheaper, shared traffic. ExpressRoute is your own private road straight to head office — pricier, but nobody else drives on it.

Application Insights

A fitness tracker for your app

It watches every heartbeat (request), every stumble (exception), and every time you call a friend for help (dependency) — then shows you the pattern so you catch problems before they become a collapse.

Azure Monitor + Log Analytics

The building's central security office

Every camera (each service) feeds footage into one control room. Azure Monitor is the whole security operation; Log Analytics is the archive room where every tape is stored and you can search "show me every camera between 2 and 3pm."
