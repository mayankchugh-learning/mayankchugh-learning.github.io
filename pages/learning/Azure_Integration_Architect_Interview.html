Cloud-agnostic map

Same integration patterns, different product names — use this when you claim multi-cloud fluency.

Talking point: I’m cloud-agnostic on the architecture. Queues, pub-sub, API façades, orchestration, and streaming are the same problems on Azure, AWS, and GCP — I map the tool to the pattern, not the brand.

Closest functional mapping for interview talk-track — not 1:1 product parity. Lead with patterns (API-led versus event-driven, orchestration versus choreography), then name the cloud service.

Questions you should ask

Turn the interview into a two-way conversation — shows architectural judgment and contract mindset.

  • What does the current integration landscape look like — greenfield, or existing APIM / Logic Apps / Service Bus to rationalise?
  • What’s driving this engagement — new programme, legacy modernisation, or production troubleshooting?
  • How is integration governance handled today — design authority already in place, or part of establishing one?
  • Who are the day-to-day stakeholders — architecture, delivery teams, or business functions (finance, HR)?
  • What does success look like at the 3-month and 6-month mark?
  • Is there an existing DevOps / CI-CD setup for integration assets, or is that in scope?
